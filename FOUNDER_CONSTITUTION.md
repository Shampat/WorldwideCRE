# WorldwideCRE Founder Constitution

## Mission

WorldwideCRE exists to make commercial real estate simpler, faster, fresher, and more trustworthy.

## Principles

1. Commercial property only.
2. Money buys exposure — not trust.
3. Listings expire automatically.
4. AI assists; humans remain responsible.
5. Never promise 100% verification or authenticity.
6. Leads belong to the listing representative.
7. WorldwideCRE does not sell individual buyer leads by default.
8. Privacy choices belong to the lister.
9. Exact, approximate, and confidential locations are supported.
10. Users must confirm they have rights or permission to publish uploaded media.
11. AI flags potential image-rights issues; it does not determine copyright ownership.
12. Shared building media is legitimate and must not be treated as duplicate abuse automatically.
13. Physical property, building, project, unit, listing, media, and user are separate concepts.
14. Multiple units in one building may have legitimate separate listings.
15. Developers and builders need bulk/project inventory tools.
16. Listing status and freshness are actively maintained.
17. Sold, leased, withdrawn, and expired properties leave normal active search.
18. Search must respect the user's requested criteria and clearly disclose relaxed criteria.
19. Nearby-market expansion should be intelligent and transparent.
20. Saved searches should provide useful alerts without spam.
21. Free accounts remain available.
22. Standard listing price is $49 CAD for 90 days.
23. Featured exposure is separate from trust and is provisionally $99 CAD for 90 days.
24. Standard refund requests have a 7-day window and manual review.
25. Country-specific currencies, units, taxes, privacy, and rules must be supported architecturally.
26. English-first launch; internationalization can come later.
27. Every feature should reduce manual work and support a fire-and-forget operating model.

## Brand Principle

**Pay for exposure. Earn trust.**

## Trust Statement

Trust scores, AI review, verification signals, and freshness indicators are signals only. They are never guarantees of authenticity, ownership, legal compliance, or transaction success.
